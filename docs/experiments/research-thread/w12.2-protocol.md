# W12.2 longitudinal Research Thread protocol

Status: **draft before freeze and measurement**

## Question

Does an explicit, append-only Research Thread help a later investigation say what
changed, what remains true, and what is still unresolved more accurately and with
less repeated work than a fresh independent investigation?

The experiment does not assume that stored memory is useful. It tests a narrower
claim: explicit links among independently preserved research roots, sources, claims,
corrections, and recheck obligations may improve follow-up research.

## Arms

- **Control:** each follow-up begins as a fresh research root. It receives the same
  current source pack, question, model route, limits, and renderer as treatment. It
  receives no hidden summary of the earlier run.
- **Treatment:** the follow-up receives the same inputs plus a validated Research
  Thread containing the prior roots and explicit source, claim, correction, and
  unresolved-question lineage. Prior prose is not evidence and the thread does not
  grant authority to a claim.

The thread record is the only intended intervention. Retrieval, current source
availability, prompts outside the intervention, model settings, budgets, and final
rendering stay matched within each pair.

## Longitudinal sequences

Freeze at least eight sequences. Every sequence starts with an initial investigation
and then one or two follow-ups. Together they cover:

1. a source whose current value genuinely changes;
2. new terminology for the same identified subject;
3. a syndicated copy that must not count as independent corroboration;
4. a real contradiction that remains visible;
5. a historical statement that remains valid only for its earlier time;
6. an initially unresolved question that later becomes answerable;
7. a deleted or link-rotated source whose old snapshot remains auditable;
8. a near-match subject that must remain separate;
9. a no-change anchor that should not manufacture novelty.

Each sequence includes independently authored expected change events, current and
historical claim states, source relationships, unresolved questions, prohibited
merges, and required abstentions. Expected answers never enter generation prompts.

## Executable boundary

`research-thread-experiment/1` records subjects with explicit identifiers, ordered
research roots, forward-only source and claim lineage, and recheck obligations. It
rejects reused snapshot or claim identities, backward lineage, unknown subjects,
and a claimed resolution without evidence. Semantic similarity alone is never a
membership basis.

Every research root survives independently. A thread is an index and lineage map;
it does not rewrite a root, replace a retained snapshot, or become a source of truth.

## Evaluation

Blind graders receive the follow-up question, the independently authored reference
ledger, the admitted current and historical sources, and one rendered answer. They
do not see the arm, thread representation, run order, or generator rationale.

Primary scores are:

- accuracy of what changed;
- accuracy of what remains currently true;
- preservation of historically true claims;
- correct handling of unresolved questions;
- decision usefulness and auditability.

Secondary measurements are repeated acquisition, duplicate claim extraction,
correct evidence reuse, time, calls, tokens, cost, freshness errors, false merges,
and stale-current leakage. Report each follow-up separately and analyze at the
sequence level rather than treating claims from one sequence as independent trials.

## Hard failures

Fail a trial if the treatment merges distinct subjects or claims, presents stale
evidence as current, loses a historically valid claim, cites a thread summary as
evidence, cannot trace a correction to its source and root, or hides an unavailable
source. Retain every failure and invalid response.

## Candidate decision rule

Freeze exact scoring and resource ceilings before model execution. The starting
adoption gate is:

- current/change-state accuracy and blind usefulness improve by at least 10
  percentage points in at least two of three repetitions;
- repeated acquisition or time to a trustworthy update falls by at least 25% on
  the target longitudinal stratum;
- zero high-impact false merges and zero stale-current leaks;
- no historical claim is silently lost or rewritten;
- no-change anchors show no material regression;
- terminal failure rates remain below 10% in every lane.

If only explicit membership and lineage links clear the gate, adopt that narrower
contract and leave automatic identity resolution out. If no treatment clears the
gate, retain independent roots and explicit comparison reports.

## Freeze and amendments

Before the first measured call, publish a manifest that pins the source revision,
case corpus, work order, thread schema, runner, validators, prompts, grader, analysis
code, model route, endpoint identity, limits, and random seed. Smoke tests live
outside the measured tree. Any material correction creates a new freeze and result
tree; it never rewrites an attempted run.
